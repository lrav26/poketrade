About

PokéTrade is a nostalgic and interactive web app where you can collect, trade, buy, and sell Pokémon cards. Whether you're a casual collector or a serious trader, PokéTrade makes it easy to grow and manage your collection. Get a random Pokémon when you sign up, explore detailed stats, send trade requests, and browse the marketplace — all in a safe, community-driven space. With a responsive design for all devices and fun features like custom nicknames, PokéTrade brings the excitement of trading Pokémon to your screen!

PokéTrade is packed with features to make collecting and trading Pokémon smooth and exciting. Users can create accounts, manage their personal inventories, view detailed Pokédex info, and nickname their Pokémon. You can send and respond to trade requests, track your trade history, and list or buy cards in the marketplace with custom pricing. Admins help keep the community safe by managing listings and users. Plus, everything works seamlessly across devices with a responsive, user-friendly design!
